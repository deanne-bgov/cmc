Why traditional ROI misses the point
A cold storage facility may not pay back on paper in the short term — but it lowers the price of tuna for every household in Isabela. When 151,297 residents pay less for a staple protein, the collective savings dwarf the capital cost. This is community infrastructure, not commercial real estate.
